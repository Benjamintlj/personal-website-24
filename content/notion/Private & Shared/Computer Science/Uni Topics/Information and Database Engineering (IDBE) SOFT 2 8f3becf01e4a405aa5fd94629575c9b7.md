# What you should know

- Explain the tasks and demonstrate understanding of the problems involved in database administration, data definition and data manipulation
- Explain the principals and evaluate the characteristics of current types of DBMSs
- Explain the use of a current structured methodology in the development of a system

- Use a DDL/DML to construct relational databases and manipulate and manage data within these databases
- Analyse data requirements and formulate solutions using a DDL/DML
- Apply techniques from a current structured methodology to the analysis and design of information systems
