# Bookshelf material

The bookshelf uses a generated walnut surface on CSS 3D furniture panels. The
books and their animations remain live components.

- Tool: built-in image generation tool; no CLI fallback.
- Website asset: `public/books/materials/walnut.jpg` (1536 × 1024).
- The generated PNG was encoded as JPEG at quality 82 for the website.
- Original: `/Users/benjaminlewis-jones/.codex/generated_images/01a09a21-c0b8-7112-9c33-19191865050d/exec-94276575-6c2b-49bb-a57e-4390dab35c44.png`.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: diffuse wood material texture for an interactive skeuomorphic bookshelf on a dark personal website. Generate one photorealistic, seamless warm American walnut wood veneer texture, landscape 1536x1024. The wood fills the complete frame, photographed perfectly orthographically head-on with no perspective. Fine natural horizontal grain, understated flowing grain, medium warm caramel-brown walnut, low contrast, carefully sanded satin finish. Elegant furniture-grade wood with delicate pores and subtle organic variation; refined and tactile, not rustic. Completely even neutral diffuse illumination, no directional highlight or shadows baked in. No furniture, no planks or gaps, no bevels, no frame, no objects, no books, no lettering, no logos or watermark. This is only a continuous material swatch, to be mapped onto code-rendered 3D shelf panels.
